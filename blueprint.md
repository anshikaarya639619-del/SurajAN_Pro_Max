# SHORTS VIRAL BLUEPRINT

## Content Growth System

### GLOBAL CREATOR FRAMEWORK

HOOK
↓
VALUE
↓
PROOF
↓
PAYOFF
↓
CTA

---

## HOOK

The opening should create a reason to continue watching.

Possible approaches:

- Curiosity
- Unexpected information
- Strong question
- Transformation
- Clear promise

---

## VALUE

Deliver useful information quickly.

Avoid unnecessary introductions.

---

## PROOF

Use:

- Examples
- Demonstrations
- Data
- Experience
- Visual evidence

when appropriate.

---

## PAYOFF

Deliver what the opening promised.

---

## CTA

Choose ONE clear action.

Examples:

- Follow
- Save
- Share
- Visit the store
- Get the guide

---

# TESTING

Do not depend on one piece of content.

Test:

- Different hooks
- Different openings
- Different thumbnails
- Different CTAs
- Different formats

Keep learning from the results.

---

# BUSINESS LAYER
