The 100-day learning path

A clear progression—not a promise of instant fluency. Each stage builds on the previous one.

Days 1–10 · First words & sentencesGreetings, introductions, simple statements, and daily repetition.
Days 11–25 · Everyday questionsAsk and answer common questions; practise useful sentence patterns.
Days 26–45 · Daily conversationsTalk about routine, people, preferences, plans, and familiar situations.
Days 46–65 · Longer responsesConnect ideas, explain choices, describe experiences, and tell short stories.
Days 66–85 · Real-life speaking practiceRole-play practical situations and practise speaking with less hesitation.
Days 86–100 · Review & speaking challengesRevisit key skills, complete practice tasks, and review your own recordings.
