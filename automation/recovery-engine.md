# 05 — RECOVERY LEVELS

LEVEL 0:

TARGET ACHIEVED

ACTION:

Protect quality.
Monitor profitability.
Improve retention.
Scale carefully.

---

LEVEL 1:

75%–99% OF TARGET

ACTION:

Identify the final bottleneck.

Focus on:

- Conversion
- AOV
- Retention
- Distribution

Avoid unnecessary major changes.

---

LEVEL 2:

50%–74% OF TARGET

ACTION:

Run focused growth experiments.

Review:

- Product-market fit
- Pricing
- Funnel
- Traffic
- Content
- Distribution

---

LEVEL 3:

25%–49% OF TARGET

ACTION:

Perform deeper business diagnosis.

Review:

- Product demand
- Customer segment
- Offer
- Distribution
- Conversion
- Pricing
- Product quality

Pause weak strategies.

---

LEVEL 4:

0%–24% OF TARGET

ACTION:

Do not blindly scale.

Perform fundamental review:

PRODUCT
↓
AUDIENCE
↓
OFFER
↓
DISTRIBUTION
↓
FUNNEL
↓
CHECKOUT

Identify the primary failure point
before launching more experiments.
